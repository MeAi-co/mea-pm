Summary Judgment for Test 5
The same fundamental split observed in Test 6 is present, and if anything more pronounced on the opening turns:

Bench C again demonstrates the failure mode of stopping at narrative sufficiency → inventing plausible-sounding insight → being forced to hedge and correct when challenged. This produces low-trust, low-value responses for any data-grounded or action-oriented use.
Bench A retrieves more aggressively, stays closer to the store, recovers cleanly when corrected, and maintains better continuity. Its extra length is the cost of higher thoroughness.

Conclusion under the agreed criteria:

For data-based reports, specific factual questions, and real-world agentic usage, Agent A remains the superior system. Bench C’s pattern of shallow retrieval + story-framing + mid-answer hedging continues to be unacceptable for production agent work.
